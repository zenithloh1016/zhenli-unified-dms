Data & settings

Secure DMS sync / standalone browser storage

When opened within the signed-in UNICOSEC DMS and its Task Hub SQL migration is installed, tasks sync privately through Supabase. Opened by itself, this page stores data only in this browser. Avoid unnecessary NRIC or sensitive personal data in task notes.

Backup and portability

Export JSON to keep all tasks, companies and activity. Import a backup to restore or move between devices.

Direct download link

On Android, choose Files or Drive from the Save / Share panel to keep your backup. A browser download may be saved under Files → Downloads. Exported files are not saved to GitHub.

Import company list / Master Listing

Select an Excel (.xlsx) or prepared JSON Master Listing directly from your Android Files picker. Imported names, registration numbers, AR anniversary, AR lodgement due dates, AFS lodgement due dates and financial year-end records populate the company register and deadline dashboard. Existing tasks and edited companies are preserved. After importing, a confirmation with the selected filename and counts appears below. No company list is sent to the website host.

Import your Master Listing.xlsx or prepared company JSON. Dates populate the statutory register. Tick the optional review-task box to create filing-status checks (not instructions to lodge). Always confirm actual lodgement status through SSM and statutory records. Do not upload confidential workbooks to public GitHub.
